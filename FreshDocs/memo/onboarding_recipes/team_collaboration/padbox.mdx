## Why This Matters

Our team uses **PadBox** as the primary platform for day-to-day research collaboration:

- Platform: [PadBox](https://padbox.research.jason-young.me/)
- Core Software: [CryptPad](https://cryptpad.org/) (open-source, free, end-to-end encrypted collaboration suite)

Most idea drafts, experiment logs, and lightweight project notes are maintained there.

---

## Before You Start

After joining the team, please proactively send your email address to **Jason**.  
He will guide account onboarding and team-space setup based on that email.

---

## Onboarding Steps

### Step 1: Accept Invitation and Register

Jason will send you a private invitation link tied to your email.

1. Open the invitation link and complete account registration.
2. You may be asked to enable 2FA during registration.
3. For 2FA, Microsoft Authenticator is a recommended option.
4. After account creation is complete, notify Jason.

### Step 2: Initial Space Provisioning (Handled by Jason)

Jason will complete two setup tasks for your account:

1. Allocate a small personal cloud quota (about 66 MB).
2. Send you a private **profile link** to his PadBox profile.

This personal quota is sufficient for most text-first research notes and idea logs.

### Step 3: Send Contact Request to Jason

1. Open Jason's Padbox profile using the profile link.
2. Click **Send a contact request** on that profile page.
3. Wait for Jason to accept your request.

### Step 4: Accept Team Invitation

After contact is established, Jason will invite you to a specific team space.

1. Check the top-right notification bell in PadBox.
2. Accept the team invitation promptly.

### Step 5: Work in Your Team Folder

After joining the team in PadBox, you should see a folder associated with your name.

1. Jason will grant you convenient edit permissions for that folder.
2. Record your research progress there according to your schedule.
3. Keep ongoing ideas, intermediate notes, and daily logs in this folder.

### Step 6: Important Visibility Rule

Content created in your **personal drive** is visible only to you by default.
Only content in the **team drive** is visible to other team members by default.
Jason will set up your team drive folder and grant appropriate permissions (Initially, only you and Jason have access).

For team collaboration, always edit and maintain shared materials in your folder under the **team drive**.

:::warning
If you accidentally write notes in your personal drive, other team members cannot see them.
Please move or recreate those notes in the team drive to keep collaboration transparent.
:::

## FAQ

### I sent a contact request but nothing changed.

This usually means the request is still pending. Wait for Jason to accept it, or send a short reminder message.

### I accepted the account invitation, but I cannot see the team yet.

Watch the top-right notification bell. Team invitations are separate from account invitations and must be accepted explicitly.

### I can log in, but I cannot edit files in my folder.

Your permission update may still be pending. Message Jason and ask him to confirm your folder permissions.

### I accidentally wrote notes in my personal drive. Can the team see them?

No. Personal-drive content is private by default. Move or recreate those notes in your team-drive folder.

### Is 66 MB enough for research work?

For text-first logs, ideas, and lightweight planning documents, yes. We encourage keeping most content in the team drive to ensure collaboration. There is usually enough space for typical research activities, including document writing, note-taking, and small data (e.g., image assets, CSV files, JSON files, Draw.io, PDF, etc.) files. If your work later requires larger shared assets, ask Jason for guidance on proper storage channels.
